# CITATION SUPPORT AUDIT — Spartina Earth v0 (M2.3b-R1)

Phase R1 (Issues #16/#14/#15), executed 2026-10-03/04. This audit
supersedes nothing in `CLAIM_SUPPORT_AUDIT.md`; it extends that audit to
the format required by the R1 mandate. Every external claim below was
checked against the cited source on a live page fetched during this
audit (publisher page, open full text, DOI-keyed bibliographic API
reproducing the publisher abstract, arXiv, or an official agency page).

**Protocol.** For each item we record: the exact manuscript claim; the
BibTeX key; full title; DOI (or agency URL for technical references); a
verbatim supporting passage actually present on a page fetched in this
audit; the source URL; and a verdict. Verdicts:

- **SUPPORTED** — the cited source states the claim (minor scope nuances
  are recorded, not hidden);
- **PARTIAL** — the source supports only part of the sentence; the
  manuscript wording is narrowed in this revision or a TODO remains;
- **UNSUPPORTED** — the source contradicts or does not contain the
  claim; the claim is removed or replaced.

Existence of a DOI is not support; no passage is quoted from memory;
where a publisher page was unreachable, the alternate host is named and
the provenance caveat is recorded. Coverage: **28 claims across 20
topics** (requirement: >=15 across >=15). Count at audit close:
26 SUPPORTED, 2 PARTIAL, 0 UNSUPPORTED. The two PARTIAL items both
received manuscript action (below and in §"Actions taken").
