\section{Zhejiang verification: data factory and the M2.1a2-R1 corrections}\label{sec:zhejiang}

\subsection{Metadata census, 1985--2026}
The data factory executes metadata-only census queries against live
catalogs with an export guard enforced at runtime and in tests. Across
three bays, six sensors, and 42 years, 294 in-service year--bay--sensor
groups produced one bounded metadata call each and 462 groups were
recorded as \texttt{SENSOR\_NOT\_OPERATIONAL} with no call; the census
retains 18{,}435 physical scene rows (Table~\ref{tab:census}). Observed
first/last scenes differ from declared mission spans---Sentinel-1 first
appears on 2015-02-09 (not 2014), Landsat~7's last observed scene is in
2023, and Yueqing Bay saw only ascending Sentinel-1 passes in
2023---which is exactly why operational availability, rather than
mission declarations, defines the observation record. In-service groups
with zero scenes (15) and with no quality-passing scenes (83) are kept
as distinct gap classes and are never interpolated.

Census geometry also exposed why a bay-wide single-scene rule fails for
tiled sensors: the best single Sentinel-2 tile covers only 0.909 of
Hangzhou Bay (tiles 51RTP/RUP/RUQ are needed) and 0.98994 of Yueqing
Bay. Multi-tile same-datatake coverage---not a lowered
threshold---is the legitimate entry point for those events.

\subsection{Real byte chain on one Sentinel-2 scene}
The export-to-checksum chain has been executed once, end to end, on a
single pre-selected Hangzhou smoke scene
(\texttt{S2B\_MSIL2A\_20200905T023549\_...\_T51RUP}; MGRS 51RUP, UTC
2020-09-05, 10\,m, four bands plus a separately exported valid mask,
EPSG:32651). Both export tasks completed, files landed with
per-file SHA-256, grid transform and bounds were re-validated against
the manifest, and the bundle records catalog fingerprint, processing
configuration hash, Git commit, and software versions. During that campaign the two predeclared windows yielded zero
eligible Landsat~8 scenes, so rather than substitute a convenient
scene we deferred both the first eligible Landsat and the first
eligible Sentinel-1 byte validation to the controlled pilot, where
they have now been executed (Section~\ref{sec:m21b}).

\subsection{Correction 1: false-zero labels and cell-edge bleed}
The initial bay-level audit reported zero SILVER label pixels in Sanmen
Bay. The R1 audit found two distinct defects. First, the stored Sanmen
geometry was an invalid \texttt{GeometryCollection}; rasterizing its
mask raised an exception that a broad handler silently converted to a
zero count. Counting the valid polygonal part yields 46{,}310 pixels,
or 41.6790\,km$^2$---the zero was a pipeline artifact, not an ecological
absence. Second, label statistics were being measured on full 10\,km
squares, so pixels outside the provisional envelope (2{,}925 in HZB,
4{,}339 in SMB, 7{,}442 in YQB at 30\,m) bled into bay totals. The
corrected v0\_1 product reports full-cell, bay-clip, and
outside-bay quantities per cell; bay-scoped SILVER-bearing 10\,km cell
counts become HZB 13, SMB 17, and YQB 11, and the per-cell clipped sums
reconcile with direct envelope intersections on all 54 size-stratified
rows (maximum discrepancy 0.026\%, within rasterization tolerance).
Cell identities were unchanged; no labels were re-adjudicated.

\subsection{Correction 2: the 2022 Sentinel-2 zero-event anomaly}
The initial pair simulation returned zero quality Sentinel-2 events for
all three bays in autumn 2022. A stage-by-stage funnel audit
(Table~\ref{tab:funnel}) showed that raw scenes, autumn scenes,
datatake groups, cell--group pairs, and coverage-passing pairs all
existed---all 25 autumn groups were genuine single-UTC-date datatakes,
and a deterministic nine-scene live metadata spot-check matched catalog
records in 9/9 cases. Failure occurred at the cloud gate, which had
combined cloud fractions over \emph{all} members of each datatake
group, including adjacent tiles that never intersect the cell. Under
the corrected contributing-scene gate, final pairs recover to 51, 150,
and 89 in HZB, SMB, and YQB, with exact bucket reconciliation
(pairs = low-coverage + coverage-passing; coverage-passing =
both-cloudy + final). Pixel-level SCL validation remains deferred and
cannot erase this geometric mis-attribution. The defect was not
specific to 2022: over the full 1985--2026 archive, 31{,}135 10\,km
pairs were wrongly rejected across all multi-frame optical sensors, and
the archive-wide quality-pair count rises from 56{,}096 to 87{,}231.
The v0 artifacts are preserved with supersession sidecars.

\subsection{The M2.1b controlled real-pixel pilot}\label{sec:m21b}
The M2.1b pilot converts the corrected contracts into real,
byte-validated observation products without crossing into modeling.
Cell selection is a deterministic, fixed-order function of tracked
label-overlap and disagreement manifests: eight Hangzhou-Bay 10\,km
cells span maximum, medium, low, and near-zero SILVER bay-clip
fractions (0.0755, 0.0457, 0.0323, 0.0299, 0.0003), the two lowest
SILVER/WEAK agreement cells (Jaccard 0.0435 and 0.5942), and one
\textsc{unlabeled} coastal control, which is treated strictly as a
control, never as a negative. Thirteen products (24 files, 121.4\,MiB;
hard cap 10\,GiB) landed under a runtime export guard with resumable,
content-addressed task handling; per-product manifests record source
scene ids, processing configuration, grid spec, per-file SHA-256,
Git commit, and software environment (Table~\ref{tab:m21b}). No label
bytes were copied, no GOLD labels exist, and no model was trained; the
pilot verifies the observation chain, not mapping accuracy, and nothing
in it demonstrates national generalization.

\paragraph{Sentinel-2: real datatakes, real multi-tile merges.}
The ten S2 products come from \emph{three} distinct datatakes, not two
as an earlier report draft stated: 2022-10-02 ($n=5$), 2022-10-10
($n=4$), and 2022-10-15 ($n=1$). Eight products are the cell primary
events and two are \textsc{extra} secondary events; both extra events
pass the frozen gates (coverage $\ge$0.99, contributing-scene cloud
$\le$0.30) but are counted only in the product and storage inventory,
never pooled into primary-event statistics. The 2022-10-15 event is a
boundary case kept deliberately: contributing cloud 0.2869 against the
0.30 gate with 0.9458 in-cell VALID fraction, attached to a cell that
also holds a clear primary. Four products are genuine same-datatake
two-tile merges (RTP+RUP, RUP+RUQ, and RUQ+RVQ); a server-side assert
pins each merge to one \texttt{DATATAKE\_IDENTIFIER} and one UTC date,
so a cross-date mosaic cannot be constructed. VALID bytes implement
policy \texttt{s2\_scl\_qa\_v1\_1} intersected with the four-band
observation mask in the source tile (revision r5); 20\,m bands are
excluded and no resampling occurs. Primary VALID fractions span
0.9610--1.0000, i.e.\ scene-level cloud metadata and pixel-level
validity now agree on real products but are not interchangeable
quantities.

\paragraph{Landsat 8/9: first eligible scenes at native 30\,m.}
The first scientifically eligible Landsat products landed for cell
E029: LC08 L2SP path/row 119/39 on 2022-09-30 (scene cloud 0.2329,
VALID 0.8116) and LC09 L2SP 118/39 on 2022-10-01 (cloud 0.0435, VALID
0.9779). Both products are 334$\times$334 pixels on one identical
30\,m grid (transform anchored at 289980\,m east, 3{,}370{,}020\,m
north, EPSG:32651), so consecutive sensor generations are
co-geolocated on a common lattice while retaining native resolution.
All seven SR bands pass 14 ordered scaling checks under the
Collection~2 coefficients (scale $2.75\times10^{-5}$, offset $-0.2$);
the raw \texttt{QA\_PIXEL} layer is exported separately as uint16
rather than baked into the imagery, and 30\,m pixels are never
regridded to 10\,m. The VALID gap between the two scenes tracks their
scene-level cloud fractions---an internal consistency check, not an
accuracy result.

\paragraph{Sentinel-1: identity decibels, independently recomputed.}
One eligible IW product landed for E029---Sentinel-1A ascending
relative orbit 171, 2022-09-17, VV/VH at 10\,m---selected strictly by
its actual per-scene geometry (coverage 1.0). Pixels are written as
the catalog's pre-converted $\sigma_0$ decibels with an identity
mapping; a second $10\log_{10}$ transform is forbidden and a
unit-envelope audit confirms it. Over the full $1000\times1000$
window, VV percentiles (p01/p50/p99) are $-24.70/-9.54/+0.79$\,dB and
VH percentiles $-28.95/-16.26/-7.70$\,dB. An independent GEE server
recompute of every landed statistic returns identical extrema and
percentile differences within 0.116\,dB (tolerance 0.6\,dB), showing
the landed file and a fresh server expression describe the same
measurement. No descending IW scene reached actual-geometry coverage
0.99 in the window; this is recorded as
\texttt{NO\_ELIGIBLE\_S1\_DESCENDING\_SCENE\_2022\_AUTUMN}, not patched
with a low-coverage pass.

\paragraph{Methodological finding: nominal MGRS frames fail production
eligibility.} Five of the eight selected cells were flagged
\texttt{V0\_1\_NOMINAL\_MGRS\_FRAME\_UNDERCOVERAGE}: the nominal MGRS
tile-frame polygons used by the corrected v0.1 archive simulator miss
each cell, whereas the real datatake footprints cover it completely and
the live, actual-geometry products above exist for all five. The
implication generalizes the Sentinel-1 footprint result: nominal
frames---representative SAR polygons \emph{and} nominal MGRS tile
polygons---are planning prefilters only. Production QA, eligibility,
and any archive statistics must use the geometry that actually
contributes pixels; an archive simulation built on nominal frames
requires an actual-geometry revision before standard-batch expansion.

\paragraph{Boundaries recorded rather than filled.} Observed tide is
\textsc{missing} for every pilot scene and FES2022b modeled tide is
\textsc{selected\_not\_deployed}; no modeled value is labeled
observed. The 303-pair standard batch and all training remain
unexecuted, and the pilot stops at its declared gate.
