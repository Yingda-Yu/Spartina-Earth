\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{No GOLD validation.}
No field, UAV, or expert-verified labels exist in the project (GOLD
= 0). All accuracy-adjacent numbers are SILVER-product reproduction or
engineering diagnostics on tiny support. GOLD acquisition is an
independent workstream with its own date, provenance, annotation,
adjudication, and permission requirements.

\paragraph{Tiny and geographically concentrated Pilot-0 support.}
The Pilot-0 test set is seven windows over five SILVER components in one
bay for one nominal year. Spatial confidence intervals are not
estimable; seed variability covers training randomness only; some model
means include collapsed runs. Pilot-0 establishes pipeline behavior,
not mapping accuracy.

\paragraph{Provisional boundaries.}
The Zhejiang bay envelopes are Tier-C provisional constructions, not
authoritative shorelines. Bay-clipped label statistics are conditional
on these envelopes and must be re-versioned if envelopes are revised.

\paragraph{Domain membership is a v1 candidate, not a frozen boundary.}
The 3{,}011/3{,}319 W10 membership result is a deterministic GIS audit
on a 100\,m$^2$ touch threshold and a 25\,km island-reach rule; ten
cells (five unattributed-land, five contested Kinmen cells) are
provisional pending documented human decisions, and the freeze review
returns \texttt{REMAIN\_PROVISIONAL}. Murray intertidal and JRC surface
water enter as target-independent context columns at 30\,km support
only---they neither decide membership nor substitute for tide
modelling---and Natural Earth attribution cannot replace boundary
authority for transboundary and contested cells. No national mapping
accuracy has been established.

\paragraph{SILVER label provenance.}
The 2015 national raster lacks documented producer, class legend,
mapping unit, exact acquisition dates, and redistribution terms; its
national area is stated in the product's UTM-zone planar convention
and is not a geodetic area. It is not comparable as change against the
2020 CM-SSM vector product even though the latter's file provenance is
now byte-verified against its official archive.

\paragraph{Pixel-level QA exists only at pilot scale.}
Scene-level cloud metadata does not establish per-pixel validity.
M2.1b executed SCL valid masks on ten real 10\,m products (VALID
0.9458--1.0000), QA\_PIXEL masks on two native-30\,m Landsat products,
and the first eligible Sentinel-1 identity-decibel validation on one
IW product with an independent server recompute. All eight cells lie in
one bay and one autumn window; archive-scale valid-pixel statistics,
the 20\,m-band resampling policy, and multi-sensor co-registration on
larger samples remain future work.

\paragraph{Tide and management evidence are missing.}
FES2022b is selected but not deployed; observed gauge coverage is
sparse and access-restricted; no modeled value may be called observed.
Zero verifiable management events with spatial ledgers were found, so
pre/post-treatment analysis cannot yet be performed.

\paragraph{Seasonal windows are hypotheses.}
The DOY windows are literature- and climatology-informed proposals
pending per-bay phenological calibration; they are not frozen policy.

\paragraph{Generalization claims are out of scope.}
Nothing here demonstrates cross-province, national, or global
performance, and no foundation-model capability is asserted. The
national design is target-independent; the metadata-only cell census
has been executed and independently re-audited (v0: 184{,}051 scenes;
v0\_1 through the 2026 YTD cutoff: 227{,}505 unique physical scenes,
93{,}035 events, 10{,}923{,}012 cell--event pairs, all 3{,}319 cells
observed, zero pixel exports), but no national pixel tier is opened,
and storage and workload byte estimates remain model assumptions
pending measured replication.
