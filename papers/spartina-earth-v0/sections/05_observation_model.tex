\section{The Spartina Earth observation model}\label{sec:model}

This section specifies the contracts that make observations comparable
across sensors. The hierarchy is regional envelope $\rightarrow$
analysis cell $\rightarrow$ observation event / acquisition group
$\rightarrow$ sensor products with quality contracts, each carrying
time, tide, label, rights, and provenance.

\subsection{Regional envelopes}
Each study region has an envelope constructed with documented
provenance---authoritative boundaries where they exist and are
licensed, otherwise a deterministic construction from cited shoreline
or hydrographic data. The three Zhejiang envelopes (Hangzhou, Sanmen,
Yueqing) are Tier-C \textbf{provisional}: reproducible locators of the
sampling frame, not authoritative scientific shorelines, and they are
not upgraded to authoritative merely because a grid has been built on
them.

\subsection{Analysis cells: identity geometry versus statistics geometry}
A fixed metric grid is anchored at the UTM zone-grid origin
(0\,m, 0\,m) in EPSG:32651; cell identifiers encode bay, size, and
integer east/north indices (e.g.\ \texttt{ZJ\_U51\_10K\_E031\_N335}),
so identities are stable across reruns. Three sizes (5, 10, and
20\,km) share the same parent lattice (Table~\ref{tab:grid}); the
10\,km size is recommended as the observation/archive unit and is
explicitly \textsc{recommended\_not\_frozen}.

Two geometries are distinguished after the M2.1a2-R1 correction.
\texttt{cell\_geometry} is the full fixed square and is the unit of
identity, archiving, and spatially disjoint splits.
\texttt{bay\_clip\_geometry} is its intersection with the provisional
envelope and is the geometry used for bay-scoped label statistics; the
complement inside the square is retained as an explicit
outside-bay quantity rather than dropped. The analysis cell is
\emph{not} a training chip: future 256/512-pixel patches will carry a
\texttt{parent\_cell\_id}, and split membership is decided at cell
level so patches cannot leak across groups.

\subsection{Observation events and acquisition groups}
An \emph{observation event} binds one cell to the set of source
acquisitions that can legitimately form one cloud-consistent view.
Optical acquisitions are grouped by real \texttt{DATATAKE\_IDENTIFIER};
tiles from the same datatake may be merged at cell level, but cross-date
mosaics are forbidden. Sentinel-1 events remain strictly single-sensor
events with ascending and descending passes, relative orbit,
polarization, and UTC preserved; passes are never mixed. Coverage
eligibility uses a fixed threshold of geometric coverage
$\ge$\,0.99 and scene-level cloud fraction $\le$\,0.30. These
thresholds were deliberately \emph{not} relaxed during the corrections
reported below; what changed was the spatial unit to which the cloud
gate is applied.

\paragraph{Contributing-scene cloud gate.} For a cell--group pair,
scene-level cloud fractions are combined only over member scenes whose
own frame geometry intersects the cell (the contributing scenes).
Taking the maximum over an entire datatake group---including adjacent
MGRS tiles that never cover the cell---was the v0 defect: a cloudy
neighboring tile poisoned eligibility for cells it did not observe. The
group-wide quantity is retained as a diagnostic column only. A
contributing scene with missing cloud metadata fails the gate and is
not rescued by clear neighbors; the gate is not applicable to SAR.

\paragraph{Pixel-level quality.} Sentinel-2 pixel QA uses policy
\texttt{s2\_scl\_qa\_v1\_1}: SCL classes 4 (vegetation), 5
(bare/not-vegetated), and 6 (water) are valid; snow/ice class~11 is
invalid. Pixel-level valid-fraction evaluation has not yet been run at
scale and is explicitly deferred to the M2.1b real-pixel stage;
scene-level cloud metadata is never presented as pixel QA.

\subsection{Sentinel-1 production geometry}
SAR eligibility cannot use a representative orbit frame. Across a
deterministic, stratified 60-scene IW audit (1{,}301 scene--cell rows;
EW open-ocean-mode frames excluded; actual per-scene geometries read in
metadata-only calls), representative-frame coverage disagrees with
measured geometry at mean absolute error 0.559 with 207 false-eligible
and 474 false-rejected pairs (Table~\ref{tab:s1footprint}), including
tens-to-200\,km track offsets and one anomalously loose representative
polygon. Policy is therefore dual: representative frames serve only as
a planning prefilter; the actual per-scene GEE geometry is the sole
production eligibility and QA geometry.

\subsection{Time, phenology, and tide}
Acquisition UTC is mandatory. The seasonal target window is a
hypothesis under calibration: the earlier DOY~260--305 autumn window
is modified to a proposed DOY~260--320 with per-bay latitudinal
calibration pending, supported by observed Spartina phenology gradients
\citep{zhang2022latitudinal} and regional cloud climatology; windows
are never chosen post hoc from model scores. Tide is represented by
four separately named fields---observed tide, modeled tide, inundation
proxy, and water-fraction proxy---with documented UTC matching
tolerances. FES2022b is \textsc{selected\_not\_deployed}; modeled or
proxy values are never called observed tide.

\subsection{Label attachment and tiers}
Each cell records label status from SILVER, WEAK, combined SILVER+WEAK,
and UNLABELED evidence computed against \texttt{bay\_clip\_geometry},
together with redistribution and internal-use rights. UNLABELED is a
sampling category for self-supervised use and control stratification,
never a negative class. A diagnostic disagreement layer records where
SILVER and WEAK masks conflict; it does not trigger relabeling, and no
layer is promoted to GOLD without independent field/UAV/expert evidence.
