\section{Data sources, label tiers, and rights}\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Earth-observation streams}
The framework ingests six primary sensors through Google Earth Engine
\citep{gorelick2017gee}: Landsat~5, 7, 8, and~9 Collection~2 Tier~1
Level-2 surface reflectance (30\,m native; the long-term stream),
Sentinel-2 \texttt{COPERNICUS/S2\_SR\_HARMONIZED}: only the four
10\,m native bands (B2/B3/B4/B8) enter the pilot products, while 20\,m
bands are excluded pending an explicit resampling policy. Sentinel-1
\texttt{COPERNICUS/S1\_GRD} IW products (C-band, VV/VH; the
collection's backscatter bands are already calibrated to decibels per
GEE product documentation, so a second $10\log_{10}$ conversion is
forbidden; peer-reviewed technical reference TODO\_CITATION). Two streams are maintained deliberately: a
30\,m Landsat-era truth stream and a 10\,m Sentinel-era stream, with
overlap years carrying both; historical Landsat pixels are never
presented as 10\,m products. Harmonized Landsat--Sentinel data
\citep{claverie2018hls} are not adopted pending a per-use scientific
justification. Sensor-quality references include Fmask-family cloud
screening \citep{zhu2015fmask} and the Sentinel-2 scene-classification
(SCL) layer.

\subsection{National label evidence: a heterogeneous product family}
National Spartina labels are audited as a family of ten acquired
products rather than as a single truth layer
(Table~\ref{tab:labelproducts}). The family comprises four GEODATA
30\,m masks (1990, 2000, 2015, 2020) delivered by the owner through the
geodata.cn order channel and byte-audited at intake; five annual CMSA
layers for 2017--2021, obtained through the NESDC purpose-registration
channel under CC BY-NC 4.0; and the sub-meter 2020 CM-SSM product. No
2010 product was verified and none is fabricated. Every product is a
SILVER external reference; none is promoted to GOLD, none is used as a
training label under current policy, and none is called ground truth.

The GEODATA masks are single-band binary rasters (positive value~1,
nodata~255, nominal 30\,m pixels). Full block-window scans independently
reproduce the value-attribute-table counts, giving 43.7652\,km$^2$
(48{,}628 pixels, 1990), 256.4343\,km$^2$ (284{,}927 pixels, 2000),
547.4583\,km$^2$ (608{,}287 pixels, 2015) and 519.8913\,km$^2$
(577{,}657 pixels, 2020) in each product's own planar convention. The
1990/2000/2020 products share a Krasovsky Albers projection but are not
pixel aligned (non-integer origin offsets and differing extents); the
2015 product is int16 in EPSG:32650 UTM~50N, so any cross-year
comparison needs explicit reprojection and harmonization. The 2015
ordered archive is byte-identical (SHA256) to the raster used earlier in
the project, which resolves the previously open relationship question;
its portal record documents Landsat-8 OLI imagery, eCognition
multiscale segmentation with object-oriented/SVM classification and
expert editing against field samples, and a reported overall accuracy
of 92\,\% that we have not independently re-derived. Exact image
acquisition dates remain \textsc{unknown}, and the portal terms limit
use to scientific research without redistribution.

The CMSA layers are EPSG:32650 polygons vectorised from a 10\,m
DeepLabv3+ raster trained on 2020 Sentinel-2 imagery and transferred to
the other years; their boundaries are not a ``10\,m geometric
resolution'' vector measurement. Our recomputation from geometry gives
positive areas (\texttt{gridcode=2}) of 494.69, 504.31, 532.99, 571.35
and 586.51\,km$^2$ for 2017--2021; small \texttt{gridcode=0} features
(0.11--6.96\,km$^2$ per year) are excluded because their semantics are
undocumented. The shipped metadata mislabels the time range as
2017--2020 and the format as GeoTIFF, although all five shapefile years
are present; both defects are reported to the provider. The GEODATA
2020 portal record is internally contradictory: its title, filename
and raster attribute table identify Spartina and its positive pixels
reach 39.35$^{\circ}$N (beyond the mangrove biogeographic range), yet
the abstract describes mangrove. We admit the delivered bytes as
Spartina on this evidence while keeping the portal conflict open.

The CM-SSM vector product for 2020 (148{,}072 single-part polygons;
59{,}370.98\,ha = 593.710\,km$^2$ recomputed from geometry, matching the
paper's 59{,}371\,ha) has \emph{verified provenance}: all eight
shapefile components of the local copy are byte-identical (SHA256) to
the official Zenodo archive (10.5281/zenodo.16296823), and identity was
established at file level, not from the coincidental area match. The
archive's rights are recorded as a source-level conflict---CC BY 4.0 on
Zenodo/DataCite versus CC BY-NC 4.0 on the NESDC mirror---and the more
permissive term is not assumed.

None of these products may be differenced into an expansion or change
series. Their datums, resolutions, mapping methods, minimum mapping
units (a 900\,m$^2$ pixel floor for GEODATA; sub-100\,m$^2$ slivers in
CM-SSM, of which 2{,}402 rings self-intersect), reference data, and
coverage all differ; the measured area steps across years and the
46.25\,km$^2$ difference between GEODATA 2015 and CM-SSM 2020 are
method/scale effects, not change estimates. A descriptive 2020
cross-product check illustrates the scale dependence: on the 10\,km
coastal-cell grid, 274 cells contain all three products and 126 contain
exactly one (72 of those GEODATA alone), and cell-level Spearman area
correlation is 0.875 between CMSA and CM-SSM but only about 0.75
between GEODATA and either finer product. This is spatial agreement
between external maps, not accuracy. The controlled harmonized
comparison has since been executed with pre-registered hypotheses on
two documented supports and is reported in
Section~\ref{sec:issue18}; it confirms moderate national agreement
(Dice 0.52--0.71) with boundary-, region- and patch-size-structured
disagreement. All products are \textsc{silver}:
credible mapping products with documented provenance, not
field-verified truth. Local rule/index-derived masks are
\textsc{weak}. GOLD field, UAV, or expert-verified labels do not yet
exist in the project (GOLD\,=\,0), and unlabeled cells are recorded as
\textsc{unlabeled}, never as negatives.

\subsection{Label rights separation}
Redistribution rights and internal model-training rights are audited
separately. The audited Zhejiang label layers are predominantly
rights-\textsc{unknown}: local possession of a file neither grants
redistribution nor permits model release. The national raster is never
copied into publicly tracked artifacts; only derived statistics and
manifests are versioned. Any GOLD acquisition must additionally carry a
permission passport distinguishing internal scientific use,
publication-derived statistics, and public redistribution.

\subsection{Environmental context layers}
Candidate context modalities are registered by scientific role rather
than ingested wholesale: elevation (Copernicus DEM), surface-water
dynamics \citep{pekel2016gsw}, intertidal extent evidence
\citep{murray2019tidalflats}, tide models (FES2014 lineage
\citep{lyard2021fes}; FES2022b selected but not deployed), and climate
reanalysis \citep{munoz2021era5land}. Inundation proxies derived from
imagery or models are labeled \texttt{proxy} and are never reported as
observed tide; observed gauge data for the study region are sparse and
partially access-restricted.
