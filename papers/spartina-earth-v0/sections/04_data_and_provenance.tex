\section{Data sources, label tiers, and rights}\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Earth-observation streams}
The framework ingests six primary sensors through Google Earth Engine
\citep{gorelick2017gee}: Landsat~5, 7, 8, and~9 Collection~2 Tier~1
Level-2 surface reflectance (30\,m native; the long-term stream),
Sentinel-2 \texttt{COPERNICUS/S2\_SR\_HARMONIZED}: only the four
10\,m native bands (B2/B3/B4/B8) enter the pilot products, while 20\,m
bands are excluded pending an explicit resampling policy. Sentinel-1
\texttt{COPERNICUS/S1\_GRD} IW products (C-band, VV/VH; the
collection's backscatter bands are already calibrated to decibels per
GEE product documentation, so a second $10\log_{10}$ conversion is
forbidden; peer-reviewed technical reference TODO\_CITATION). Two streams are maintained deliberately: a
30\,m Landsat-era truth stream and a 10\,m Sentinel-era stream, with
overlap years carrying both; historical Landsat pixels are never
presented as 10\,m products. Harmonized Landsat--Sentinel data
\citep{claverie2018hls} are not adopted pending a per-use scientific
justification. Sensor-quality references include Fmask-family cloud
screening \citep{zhu2015fmask} and the Sentinel-2 scene-classification
(SCL) layer.

\subsection{National label evidence: a heterogeneous product family}
National Spartina labels are audited as a family of ten verified
products rather than as a single truth layer
(Table~\ref{tab:labelproducts}). The family spans the GEODATA 30\,m
series (1990 and 2000 exist as order-gated portal records; 2015 is held
locally; a 2020 30\,m record carries a title-versus-abstract metadata
contradiction; no 2010 product was verified and none is fabricated),
five annual 10\,m CMSA DeepLabv3+ layers for 2017--2021 available only
behind purpose registration under CC BY-NC 4.0, and one sub-meter 2020
product (CM-SSM). Each product is assigned an intended role---SILVER
reference, external label, or high-resolution validation
reference---and no product is promoted to GOLD.

The principal local SILVER label remains the packaged 30\,m raster
nominally dated 2015 (int16, EPSG:32650, nodata 255, value set
$\{1\}$). Independent windowed recounts reproduce its
value-attribute-table count: 608{,}287 positive pixels, i.e.\
547.4583\,km$^2$ in the product's UTM-50N planar convention; its
producer, class legend, exact acquisition dates, and redistribution
terms remain undocumented open items. The CM-SSM vector product for
2020 (148{,}072 single-part polygons; 59{,}370.98\,ha = 593.710\,km$^2$
recomputed from geometry, matching the paper's 59{,}371\,ha) now has
\emph{verified provenance}: all eight shapefile components of the local
copy are byte-identical (SHA256) to the official Zenodo archive
(10.5281/zenodo.16296823), and identity was established at file level,
not from the coincidental area match. The archive's rights are
recorded as a source-level conflict---CC BY 4.0 on Zenodo/DataCite
versus CC BY-NC 4.0 on the NESDC mirror---and the more permissive term
is not assumed.

The 2015 raster and the 2020 CM-SSM vector must not be differenced into
a 2015$\rightarrow$2020 expansion. Their resolution (30\,m versus
0.9\,m), mapping method (OBIA/SVM with expert editing versus
multi-scale OSPPF object-based segmentation, Random Forest and manual
refinement on 0.9\,m Google Earth RGB plus 15 Sentinel-2 phenological
bands), minimum mapping units (900\,m$^2$ pixel floor versus
sub-100\,m$^2$ slivers; 67.8\,\% of CM-SSM features are below
100\,m$^2$ and 2{,}402 rings self-intersect), reference data, and
province coverage all differ. The descriptive difference of
46.25\,km$^2$ is therefore not a change estimate; harmonization would
be required before any temporal interpretation. Both products are
\textsc{silver}: credible mapping products with documented provenance
gaps, not field-verified truth. Local rule/index-derived masks are
\textsc{weak}. GOLD field, UAV, or expert-verified labels do not yet
exist in the project (GOLD\,=\,0), and unlabeled cells are recorded as
\textsc{unlabeled}, never as negatives.

\subsection{Label rights separation}
Redistribution rights and internal model-training rights are audited
separately. The audited Zhejiang label layers are predominantly
rights-\textsc{unknown}: local possession of a file neither grants
redistribution nor permits model release. The national raster is never
copied into publicly tracked artifacts; only derived statistics and
manifests are versioned. Any GOLD acquisition must additionally carry a
permission passport distinguishing internal scientific use,
publication-derived statistics, and public redistribution.

\subsection{Environmental context layers}
Candidate context modalities are registered by scientific role rather
than ingested wholesale: elevation (Copernicus DEM), surface-water
dynamics \citep{pekel2016gsw}, intertidal extent evidence
\citep{murray2019tidalflats}, tide models (FES2014 lineage
\citep{lyard2021fes}; FES2022b selected but not deployed), and climate
reanalysis \citep{munoz2021era5land}. Inundation proxies derived from
imagery or models are labeled \texttt{proxy} and are never reported as
observed tide; observed gauge data for the study region are sparse and
partially access-restricted.
