\section{National scaling design}\label{sec:national}

This section describes the \emph{mainland China coastal domain v0}:
the administrative clip excludes Taiwan and the audited national label
products are themselves mainland-China products, so the present scope is
never described as the whole China coast. The domain and the 10\,km
lattice are built, but both remain \texttt{PROVISIONAL\_NOT\_FROZEN};
no national pixel export and no national result map exists at the time
of writing, and the national EO metadata census (Issue \#16) is
executed separately after this manuscript version.
The central design rule is that the study domain must be independent of
the target. Restricting the domain to 2015 positive Spartina cells
would systematically exclude later expansion, eradicated sites,
recurrence, previously omitted populations, and true unlabeled controls.

\subsection{Target-independent coastal domain and cells}
The domain is built from independent coastal geospatial evidence
rather than from Spartina labels: land is the Natural Earth 10\,m
admin-0 China polygon (v5.1.1, public domain; Taiwan is a separate row
and excluded by design), the coastline and small-island set come from
GSHHG~2.3.7 (L1, high resolution; LGPL-3.0-or-later), and the
analysis corridor is a geometric buffer on land and sea with inland
foreign-border segments removed. A fixed rule recovers 3{,}093
nearshore islands $\leq$100\,km$^2$ (1{,}898\,km$^2$) whose
representative point lies within 25\,km of the China polygon and
outside every other admin-0 polygon---archipelago coast (Zhoushan,
Miaodao, Changshan, Wanshan) that the 1:10\,m administrative layer
omits. Cell retention requires at least 1\,\% corridor intersection,
which removes triple-border slivers.

Two deterministic lattices were compared: a national equal-area China
Albers conic grid (ESRI:102025 parameters, WGS84), in which every
10\,km cell covers exactly $10^{8}$\,m$^2$, and UTM-zone-aware 10\,km
grids in zones 49--52. The UTM scheme double-covers the coast at the
114$^{\circ}$E seam (75 overlapping cell pairs, 2{,}038\,km$^2$ of
pairwise overlap) and at 120$^{\circ}$E (301 pairs, 8{,}026\,km$^2$),
with no overlap at 126$^{\circ}$E; the Albers lattice is seam-free and
is therefore selected as the canonical archive lattice, while UTM
remains a sensor-native check only. At the provisional 10\,km corridor
width the canonical lattice contains 3{,}319 coastal cells over a
235{,}984\,km$^2$ corridor; the 5\,km and 20\,km a-priori sweeps give
2{,}542 and 4{,}767 cells. Full-resolution GSHHG changes the counts by
at most +6 cells ($\leq$0.16\,\%), and omitting island recovery
removes 12.3\,\% of cells, so the lattice is coastline-resolution
insensitive but materially dependent on the island rule, which is kept
strictly geometric. Each cell carries a deterministic reversible
identifier (projection, zone where applicable, row, column), geometry
hash, and coastal-relevance metadata; label-derived entry conditions
are forbidden.

\subsection{Label-overlay flags and exclusive strata}
Labels enter the domain strictly as overlays. Each cell carries five
\emph{independent boolean flags}---2015 SILVER positive, 2015 nearby
(one fixed 10\,km lattice ring), CM-SSM 2020 positive, CMSA history
available (not built: the archive is registration-gated), and
unlabeled in the products obtained---so overlapping evidence is not
collapsed. An exclusive priority stratum is derived from the flags for
sampling design; at the provisional width 406 cells intersect the 2015
raster, 812 additional cells are 2015-nearby-only, 385 cells intersect
CM-SSM 2020, and 482 cells are positive in at least one obtained
product. The remaining 2{,}913 cells are unlabeled coastal cells: this
is absence of evidence in the products obtained, never an ecological
negative. Management-evidence and GOLD strata remain designed but not
built (no verifiable spatial management ledger exists; GOLD\,=\,0).

\subsection{Metadata census through footprint indexing}
The national census is metadata-only by construction and forbids
cell-by-scene \texttt{getInfo} brute force: static WRS-2 path/row
frames for Landsat and MGRS tile frames for Sentinel-2 act as cheap
prefilters, Sentinel-1 eligibility uses batched \emph{actual}
per-scene geometry (representative frames are never the final gate,
following the Issue \#13 result), and all joins are evaluated locally.
Recorded fields cover scene/event counts, UTC, coverage, full-year and
candidate-season tags, source QA, pass/orbit, datatake/tiles, and
path/row, with sensor-era status distinguished as not-operational,
no-scenes, no-cell-intersection, metadata-available, or query-failed.
The census \emph{design and contracts} are versioned, but national
scene/event counts do not exist yet: the census is executed under
Issue~\#16, and no national availability number is anticipated in this
paper.

\subsection{Auxiliary registry and storage tiers}
Thirteen context and sensor sources are registered with explicit
CORE / OPTIONAL / OPTIONAL\_CONTEXT / VALIDATION\_ONLY / BLOCKED roles,
including Murray intertidal, JRC surface water, the NESDC 2020 tidal-flat
collection (registration-gated), Copernicus GLO-30 (license-gated),
SRTM, JAXA ALOS/PALSAR annual mosaics (25\,m; 2011--2014 gap; DN is not
$\gamma^0$), ERA5-Land, FES2022b and TPXO9 (modeled tide only), and a
management-events registry that is blocked pending real spatial ledgers.
Four storage tiers bound future work with M2.1b-calibrated byte
assumptions: Tier~0 metadata for all 3{,}319 cells over 42 archive
years (139{,}398 cell-years; 0.53\,GiB); Tier~1 positive-history cells
only (482 cells, $\approx$83\,GiB, $\approx$289 platform task-hours);
Tier~2 one standard monitoring year on all cells ($\approx$572\,GiB,
$\approx$1{,}991 task-hours); Tier~3 the full candidate archive
($\approx$134\,TiB, $\approx$460{,}000 task-hours). These are
model estimates, not measurements; per-sensor national availability is
explicitly pending the Issue~\#16 census and is not anticipated here.
No tier that exports pixels is opened in this study.
