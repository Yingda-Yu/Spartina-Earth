\section{National scaling design}\label{sec:national}

This section describes the design for a China-scale frame; no national
pixel export and no national result map exists at the time of writing.
The central design rule is that the study domain must be independent of
the target. Restricting the domain to 2015 positive Spartina cells
would systematically exclude later expansion, eradicated sites,
recurrence, previously omitted populations, and true unlabeled controls.

\subsection{Target-independent coastal domain and cells}
The national candidate domain \texttt{CHINA\_COASTAL\_DOMAIN\_V0} is
built from independent coastal evidence---reproducible coastline, a
fixed coastal corridor, and intertidal/wetland context where licensing
permits---rather than from Spartina labels. The fixed 10\,km archive
cell is reused wherever possible on zone-appropriate metric grids, each
cell carrying a stable identifier, geometry hash, coastal-relevance
flag, province/coastal-segment metadata, and (never) a label-derived
entry condition. Mainland versus island scope is documented from the
chosen geospatial source, not from political prose.

\subsection{2015 SILVER stratification}
The 2015 national raster is overlaid onto national cells strictly as a
SILVER occupancy prior: positive pixels, positive area, positive
fraction, and distance to the nearest 2015 positive patch. Cells are
stratified into SILVER\_2015\_POSITIVE, SILVER\_2015\_NEARBY,
UNLABELED\_COASTAL, MANAGEMENT\_EVIDENCE\_AVAILABLE, and
GOLD\_EVIDENCE\_TARGET (a candidate designation only). A zero overlay
is not interpreted as confirmed absence: the source's zero semantics do
not support that reading, and the background is nodata rather than an
explicit absence class. A priority corridor for future export may be
constructed from the SILVER prior plus coastal buffers and historical
ecological evidence, but it never replaces the full coastal domain.

\subsection{Metadata census through footprint indexing}
The national census is metadata-only and avoids cell-by-scene
\texttt{getInfo} brute force: WRS-2 path/row indices for Landsat, MGRS
tile indices for Sentinel-2, and actual per-scene Sentinel-1 geometry
indices drive offline scene-footprint to cell joins. Recorded fields
include scene/event counts, UTC, coverage, season availability, QA
level, pass/orbit, datatake/tiles, and path/row per cell, year, and
sensor over 1985--2026. Auxiliary modalities are registered with a
scientific purpose, resolutions, coverage years, license, platform
availability, production difficulty, and a CORE / OPTIONAL /
VALIDATION\_ONLY / BLOCKED role.

\subsection{Tiers and estimates}
Four tiers bound future work: Tier~0 metadata census only; Tier~1
2015-positive and nearby pilot archive; Tier~2 standard national
monitoring set; Tier~3 full coastal candidate archive. Product counts,
storage, and platform workload are estimated and approved before any
pixel export tier is opened.
