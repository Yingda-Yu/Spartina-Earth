\section{National scaling design}\label{sec:national}

This section keeps two objects explicitly separate: the immutable
10\,km \emph{grid} (3{,}319 cells with fixed geometry and reversible
identifiers) and \emph{mainland coastal-domain membership}, a
versioned attribute on that cell-id space. The two are not the same:
grid cells intersected by the geometric corridor may still fall over
foreign land or open sea. The administrative clip excludes Taiwan and
the audited national label products are themselves mainland-China
products, so the present scope is never described as the whole China
coast. The evidence chain is therefore: independent mainland coastal
evidence $\rightarrow$ deterministic domain membership $\rightarrow$
stable W10 grid cells $\rightarrow$ observation events. The grid, the
v0 domain and the v1 membership candidate all remain
\texttt{PROVISIONAL\_NOT\_FROZEN}; the accompanying freeze review
returns the verdict \texttt{REMAIN\_PROVISIONAL} pending closure of ten
cells. No national pixel export and no national result map exists. The national EO
metadata census (Issue~\#16) has, however, now been executed twice as a
metadata- and geometry-only operation---v0 (1984--2025) and the audited
revision v0\_1, which extends through the frozen year-to-date cutoff
2026-10-03T00:00:00Z---reading live GEE catalogs with zero pixel
exports under an installed \texttt{ee.batch.Export} guard. The v0
product files are untouched; v0\_1 supersedes them through an explicit
supersession manifest.
The central design rule is that the study domain must be independent of
the target. Restricting the domain to 2015 positive Spartina cells
would systematically exclude later expansion, eradicated sites,
recurrence, previously omitted populations, and true unlabeled controls.

\subsection{Target-independent coastal domain and cells}
The domain is built from independent coastal geospatial evidence
rather than from Spartina labels: land is the Natural Earth 10\,m
admin-0 China polygon (v5.1.1, public domain; Taiwan is a separate row
and excluded by design), the coastline and small-island set come from
GSHHG~2.3.7 (L1, high resolution; LGPL-3.0-or-later), and the
analysis corridor is a geometric buffer on land and sea with inland
foreign-border segments removed. A fixed rule recovers 3{,}093
nearshore islands $\leq$100\,km$^2$ (1{,}898\,km$^2$) whose
representative point lies within 25\,km of the China polygon and
outside every other admin-0 polygon---archipelago coast (Zhoushan,
Miaodao, Changshan, Wanshan) that the 1:10\,m administrative layer
omits. Cell retention requires at least 1\,\% corridor intersection,
which removes triple-border slivers.

Two deterministic lattices were compared: a national equal-area China
Albers conic grid (ESRI:102025 parameters, WGS84), in which every
10\,km cell covers exactly $10^{8}$\,m$^2$, and UTM-zone-aware 10\,km
grids in zones 49--52. The UTM scheme double-covers the coast at the
114$^{\circ}$E seam (75 overlapping cell pairs, 2{,}038\,km$^2$ of
pairwise overlap) and at 120$^{\circ}$E (301 pairs, 8{,}026\,km$^2$),
with no overlap at 126$^{\circ}$E; the Albers lattice is seam-free and
is therefore selected as the canonical archive lattice, while UTM
remains a sensor-native check only. At the provisional 10\,km corridor
width the canonical lattice contains 3{,}319 coastal cells over a
235{,}984\,km$^2$ corridor; the 5\,km and 20\,km a-priori sweeps give
8{,}192 and 1{,}383 Albers cells (8{,}968 and 1{,}557 UTM-zone-aware
cells). Full-resolution GSHHG changes the counts by
at most +6 cells ($\leq$0.16\,\%), and omitting island recovery
removes 12.3\,\% of cells, so the lattice is coastline-resolution
insensitive but materially dependent on the island rule, which is kept
strictly geometric. Each cell carries a deterministic reversible
identifier (projection, zone where applicable, grid-width token, row,
column), geometry hash, and coastal-relevance metadata; label-derived
entry conditions are forbidden.

\subsection{v1 membership audit on the immutable grid}
Because corridor intersection is not membership, Issue~\#17 audited all
3{,}319 v0 cells with a deterministic, target-independent rule applied
to per-cell GIS evidence only (GSHHG land/coastline, Natural Earth
ownership, nearest-landfall distances); labels, scene and event counts,
model outputs, and the Murray/JRC layers are structurally incapable of
entering a decision. Ownership first normalises the admin units: the
Hong Kong and Macao map units are folded into China (a literal
name-based reading had falsely treated 23 Pearl River delta cells as
foreign), whereas the Taiwan-administered units encountered inside the
corridor---the Kinmen/Matsu archipelago---are marked contested rather
than silently claimed or deleted. The ordered rule keeps any cell with
$\geq$100\,m$^2$ of Chinese mainland land (mixed transboundary cells
are kept with the foreign share recorded), keeps cells anchored by a
nearshore island only when the Chinese mainland is the strictly nearest
administered landfall (refining the v0 island rule, which admitted
foreign-landfall islets near the Tumen triple border), excludes
foreign-only land, foreign-nearer water, and water beyond the 10\,km
landfall reach, applies a 500\,m border-tie band, and leaves
incomplete or sovereignty-ambiguous cells provisional.

Of the 3{,}319 v0 cells, the v1 candidate keeps 3{,}011 (2{,}752
mainland-coastal, 259 island-coastal; nominal footprint
301{,}100\,km$^2$, $-$30{,}800\,km$^2$ against v0), excludes 298 as
domain artifacts, and holds 10 as provisional. All 20 Issue~\#16
flagged cells are independently confirmed as artifacts (17 over
Russian, Korean or Vietnamese administered land; 3 open-sea edge
cells), while the three \texttt{INDEX\_MISS} cells are genuine Chinese
mainland. The full-population scan exposed 278 further artifacts that
the manual audit could not see: 256 open-sea corridor slivers,
additional foreign coast slivers, and two foreign-nearer water cells.
The ten provisional cells split into five with material unattributed
land (Pearl estuary, the Matsu channel, and three remote Zhejiang
island groups 18--27\,km offshore) and five Kinmen cells within
0.9--9\,km of Fujian; each is listed in the supersession manifest and
must receive a documented human decision before any freeze. The
a-priori width sweep is stable in character rather than borderline:
artifact rates are 11.45\,\% at W5, 8.98\,\% at W10 and 7.74\,\% at W20,
with island-only fragmentation of 9.67\,\%, 7.95\,\% and 6.65\,\%; W20
dilutes the same artifacts over larger cells rather than removing them,
so the W10 choice is supported rather than overturned.

Murray intertidal v1.1 (11 epochs, 1984--2016; CC-BY-4.0;
\citealp{murray2019tidalflats}) and JRC
Global Surface Water v1.4 (1984--2021; Copernicus, attribution
``Source: EC JRC/Google''; \citealp{pekel2016gsw}) were fetched as batched server-side scalar
summaries for all cells (166 \texttt{reduceRegions} calls at 30\,km
analysis scale; no national image export) and are joined as descriptive
context columns only. At that scale the Murray product is a coarse
support indicator---it is positive in 2{,}867 of the 3{,}011 kept cells
but also in 249 of the 298 excluded cells---which is precisely why it
cannot measure cell-level tidal-flat area and is barred from membership
decisions; JRC surface water is reported as context and is not a tide
proxy. Excluded cells are never deleted from the grid registry: all
3{,}319 rows persist with a versioned membership token, evidence JSON,
and the pinned v0 registry SHA-256.

\subsection{Label-overlay flags and exclusive strata}
Labels enter the domain strictly as overlays. Each cell carries seven
\emph{orthogonal boolean flags}---2015 SILVER positive (406 cells),
CM-SSM 2020 positive (385), 2015 nearby (one fixed 10\,km lattice band;
812), multi-product positive (both products; 309), plus three
schema-reserved flags that are empty at v0: GOLD evidence, CMSA history
(the archive is registration-gated), and management evidence---so
overlapping evidence is never collapsed. The overlays are computed on
the immutable v0 grid (3{,}319 cells); for sampling we additionally
derive one \emph{exclusive priority stratum} per cell, whose counts
partition the grid exactly: 309 multi-product positive, 97 positive
only in 2015, 76 positive only in 2020, 742 near-positive unlabeled,
and 2{,}095 other coastal unlabeled cells (482 cells positive in at
least one obtained product). Restricted to the 3{,}011 active v1
member cells the same strata partition as 308, 97, 76, 724 and 1{,}806:
no excluded artifact cell carries any SILVER positive label, and 481
of the 482 positive cells are active members; the remaining
multi-product positive cell sits on a provisional Kinmen cell and is
held out of sampling pending the ownership decision rather than being
silently used. The cells without positive evidence are absence of
evidence in the products obtained, never an ecological negative.
Management-evidence and GOLD strata remain designed but not built (no
verifiable spatial management ledger exists; GOLD\,=\,0).

\subsection{Metadata census through footprint indexing}
The national census is metadata-only by construction and forbids
cell-by-scene \texttt{getInfo} brute force: static WRS-2 path/row
frames for Landsat and MGRS tile frames for Sentinel-2 act as cheap
prefilters, Sentinel-1 eligibility uses batched \emph{actual}
per-scene geometry (representative frames are never the final gate,
following the Issue \#13 result), and all joins are evaluated locally.
Recorded fields cover scene/event counts, UTC, coverage, full-year and
candidate-season tags, source QA, pass/orbit, datatake/tiles, and
path/row, with sensor-era status distinguished as not-operational,
no-scenes, no-cell-intersection, metadata-available, or query-failed.
The executed v0 census (1984--2025; S2 from 2015 and S1 from late
2014) required 85 batched optical and 30 Sentinel-1 metadata calls (no
per-cell calls; three oversized yearly S1 ascending batches were
re-fetched as half-year scopes) and returned 184{,}051 unique physical
scenes: 21{,}022 Landsat~5, 20{,}187 Landsat~7, 13{,}306 Landsat~8,
4{,}265 Landsat~9, 108{,}232 Sentinel-2 granules and 17{,}039
Sentinel-1 scenes. The R1 integrity audit then rebuilt the census as
v0\_1 with four documented change reasons
(\texttt{CHINA\_EO\_CENSUS\_SUPERSESSION\_v0\_1.json}; v0 files
untouched). (1)~\emph{2026 YTD.} Only operational sensors were queried
for 2026 and the year is marked \texttt{PARTIAL\_YEAR} through the
frozen cutoff---855 Landsat~8, 888 Landsat~9, 14{,}463 Sentinel-2 and
1{,}255 Sentinel-1 YTD scenes; Landsat~5 is recorded
\texttt{SENSOR\_NOT\_OPERATIONAL} without a query and Landsat~7 has zero
2026 rows after imaging was suspended on 2024-01-19
\citep{usgs2024l7extended}. Partial-year rows never enter full-year
comparisons or chronic-gap flags. (2)~\emph{Sentinel-1 pass audit.}
The S1 universe was rebuilt from an unfiltered national pull (33
batched calls) with ascending and descending scenes retained at every
funnel stage; the v0\_1 set is 18{,}891 actual-footprint IW/VV$|$VH
scenes intersecting W10 cells. The audited diagnosis token is
\texttt{DESCENDING\_REGIONALLY\_AND\_TEMPORALLY\_IMBALANCED}:
descending observations exist nationally---22{,}926 raw and 3{,}145
cell-intersecting scenes in 2015--2025, and 281{,}321 descending
cell--event pairs---but availability is strongly region- and
period-dependent. Pass availability was substantial in parts of
Bohai (about 50\,\% descending share, e.g.\ 200 of 318 scenes at one
audited Bohai cell) but much sparser along southern coastal segments
(below 5\,\% in the Yangtze, Zhejiang--Fujian, Pearl River Delta and
Hainan spot checks), and the national descending share fell sharply
to 4.8\,\% in 2022--2025 after the Sentinel-1B anomaly of 2021-12-23
and its declared end of mission on 2022-08-03
\citep{copernicus2022s1b}. The complete audited era is reported
explicitly: the partial 2014 record (pull starts 2014-10-01; raw
ascending/descending scenes exist but none intersects a W10 cell),
the 2015--2025 full years, and 2026 YTD as \texttt{PARTIAL\_YEAR}
without annualization. Ten deterministic direct-GEE spot checks
(at least two per region) confirm the real, region- and era-structured
acquisition pattern rather than a pipeline filter---the v0 cache itself
retained 2{,}763 descending scenes. (3)~\emph{NE edge audit.} The 23
far-northeast cells (lon~130.4--131.2, lat~41.9--42.4) that v0
reported chronic-zero for every common-era sensor were audited case by
case: none was a query failure; 3 are \texttt{INDEX\_MISS} (the v0
server bbox stopped at 127$^{\circ}$E although the domain reaches
132$^{\circ}$E), 6 are \texttt{DOMAIN\_EDGE\_ARTIFACT} and 14 are
\texttt{MIXED\_ADMIN\_COAST\_ARTIFACT}. The footprint indices were
expanded to 71 WRS-2 frames ($+5$) and 141 MGRS tiles ($+13$), the
affected historical ranges were re-queried (88 successful calls,
including half-year and quarter rescue splits), and all 23 cells are
observed in v0\_1; there are zero optical or Sentinel-2 chronic zeros.
Only 7 chronic-zero cells remain, all Sentinel-1-only island/border
cells at the Beibu Gulf--Paracel margin with no IW/VV$|$VH footprint
intersection in any year; they are kept as an explicit gap, never
patched. (4)~\emph{L7 extended mission preserved.} All 20{,}187 v0
Landsat~7 rows are preserved. Nominal-repeat-orbit rows join through
WRS-2 (21{,}132 distinct nominal events in v0\_1); the 6{,}156 pulled
Extended Science Mission scenes (2022-05-05 to 2024-01-15; 1{,}418
intersecting W10 cells) are flagged
\texttt{OFF\_NOMINAL\_EXTENDED\_MISSION} with
\texttt{default\_production\_eligible=false}, are joined to cells via
their actual pulled footprints
(\texttt{L7\_EXTENDED\_ACTUAL\_FOOTPRINT}; 1{,}418 distinct events,
122{,}744 cell--event pairs) and are reported separately from nominal
totals, consistent with the USGS note that no U.S.\ ARD or Level-3
products were produced from this mission phase
\citep{usgs2024l7extended}; nine scenes from the 2022-04 orbit-lowering
standby window are preserved as metadata without pairs.
Sentinel-2 granules from the same datatake on the same day are grouped
into one observation event, and local geometric joins on v0\_1 yield
227{,}505 unique physical scenes, 93{,}035 distinct events
(L5~23{,}382; L7~22{,}550, of which 1{,}418 extended; L8~15{,}944;
L9~5{,}737; S1~18{,}891; S2~6{,}531), 10{,}923{,}012 cell--event pairs,
and all 3{,}319 cells observed by at least one sensor in the
2015--2025 common era (v0: 3{,}296). The census is enumerated over the
immutable v0 grid, not over the membership result: events on the 298
excluded cells remain recorded for provenance, and membership acts as a
read-time filter rather than a deletion; the 3{,}011 active members are
likewise all observed in 2015--2025. Median full-year observation
density at W10 is 20 Landsat~8, 143 Sentinel-2 and 40 Sentinel-1 events
per cell-year; at W10, 80\,\%/76\,\%/78\,\% of cell--event pairs fully
cover the cell for L8/S2/S1, so the nominal-frame prefilter is never
treated as full-cover evidence. All 1{,}190 island-only, 1{,}570
mainland and 559 mixed cells are observed in 2015--2025 (v0:
98.3\,\%/99.8\,\%/100\,\%). The full-year gap matrix now partitions
into 16{,}466 no-chronic-gap, 3{,}319 not-applicable (pre-operational
Landsat~5 cell-years), 122 multi-year-gap and 7 Sentinel-1-only
chronic-zero cell--sensor rows. Census contracts, checksums, per-call
resource ledgers (241 batched catalog calls across the five v0/v0\_1
ledgers, plus the deterministic direct spot checks) and the full
availability/gap matrices are versioned as run manifest
\texttt{EO\_CENSUS\_RUN\_MANIFEST\_v0.json} together with
\texttt{CHINA\_EO\_CENSUS\_SUPERSESSION\_v0\_1.json}; no census bytes
are tracked in Git. A deterministic 20-cell first-pixel panel (five strata
and four geometry-edge quotas, SHA-256 ranked on cell IDs) is designed
but explicitly \emph{not executed}: no pixel download has been
authorized. The panel was re-validated against the v1 membership audit
and all 20 cells are active members (19 mainland-coastal
decisions---18 land-touching and one Chinese-side seawater cell---and
one nearshore-island cell), so the design needs no revision when
execution is later authorized.

\subsection{Auxiliary registry and storage tiers}
Thirteen context and sensor sources are registered with explicit
CORE / OPTIONAL / OPTIONAL\_CONTEXT / VALIDATION\_ONLY / BLOCKED roles,
including Murray intertidal, JRC surface water, the NESDC 2020 tidal-flat
collection (registration-gated), Copernicus GLO-30 (license-gated),
SRTM, JAXA ALOS/PALSAR annual mosaics (25\,m; 2011--2014 gap; DN is not
$\gamma^0$), ERA5-Land, FES2022b and TPXO9 (modeled tide only), and a
management-events registry that is blocked pending real spatial ledgers.
Four storage tiers bound future work with M2.1b-calibrated byte
assumptions: Tier~0 metadata for all 3{,}319 cells over 42 archive
years (139{,}398 cell-years; 0.53\,GiB); Tier~1 positive-history cells
only (482 cells, $\approx$83\,GiB, $\approx$289 platform task-hours);
Tier~2 one standard monitoring year on all cells ($\approx$572\,GiB,
$\approx$1{,}991 task-hours); Tier~3 the full candidate archive
($\approx$134\,TiB, $\approx$460{,}000 task-hours). These are
model estimates, not measurements; per-sensor national availability is
now measured by the Issue~\#16 metadata census (reported above) and
can later replace the calibrated assumptions, but no tier that exports
pixels is opened in this study.

\subsection{Pre-registered three-product disagreement audit for 2020}
\label{sec:issue18}
The 2020 layer of each acquired product family---GEODATA (30\,m raster;
519.89\,km$^2$ native), CMSA 2020 (\texttt{gridcode=2}; 571.35\,km$^2$)
and CM-SSM 2020 (593.71\,km$^2$)---was compared under a protocol whose
six hypotheses and decision thresholds were fixed before any result was
read (Issue~\#18). No product is treated as truth: with GOLD\,=\,0 the
output is an agreement characterisation, and GEODATA serves only as a
comparison support. The pre-registered common 5\,m lattice was abandoned
before execution because transferring a 30\,m observation onto 5\,m
would fabricate thirty-six binary observations per real pixel and
because no product natively observes at 5\,m. Two documented supports
replace it: the GEODATA native 30\,m grid, on which the vectors
contribute exact per-pixel fractional cover (all three pairs), and a
10\,m project comparison lattice that is not claimed to reconstruct
CMSA's native pixel grid (CMSA vs CM-SSM only). Block stripes carry
halos so the 300\,m boundary-distance transform never meets a block
edge; nearest-neighbour reads and centre containment are used for hit
masks, and area transfers reproduce native totals within 0.4\,\%.
Geometry repair is applied to derived copies only: 499 of 8{,}864 CMSA
features and 2{,}402 of 148{,}072 CM-SSM features were self-intersecting
rings (with 22 and 100{,}455 sub-100\,m$^2$ slivers respectively);
MakeValid repair dropped no feature and changed total area by 0.0\,\% in
both products. The 50 CMSA \texttt{gridcode=0} features remain
\textsc{unknown} and were never relabelled negative.

National totals are similar while spatial agreement is only moderate
(Table~\ref{tab:labeldisagreement2020}): full-extent binary Dice is
0.620 for GEO--CMSA, 0.522 for GEO--CM-SSM and 0.715 for CMSA--CM-SSM,
and W10 cluster-bootstrap intervals stay at or below 0.76 for every
pair. The six pre-registered decisions are: H1 boundary concentration
\emph{supported}---disagreement at 0--30\,m is 7.7$\times$, 5.9$\times$
and 5.9$\times$ the 120--300\,m rate for the three pairs (8.2$\times$
for CMSA--CM-SSM on the 10\,m lattice) and decays monotonically across
the distance bands; H2 fine-patch discordance \emph{not supported},
with the effect in the opposite direction (0.210 inside CM-SSM patches
$<$2{,}500\,m$^2$ vs 0.316 inside $\geq$10$^5$\,m$^2$ meadows); H3
coarse-support absence in small patches \emph{supported}---89.2\,\% of
100--900\,m$^2$ CMSA patches and 86.5\,\% of CM-SSM patches carry zero
GEODATA cover, against 17.7\,\% and 23.6\,\% of patches
$\geq$10$^5$\,m$^2$, with monotone ordering across size classes; H4
regional structure \emph{supported}---regional Dice ranges are 0.68,
0.68 and 0.45 (0.43 on the 10\,m lattice), from 0.04 for GEO--CM-SSM in
one province to 0.81; H5 area-vs-patch divergence \emph{supported}
(8.6\,\% national area bias at 0.620 Dice); and H6 binary-labelling loss
\emph{supported}---36.6\,\% and 42.5\,\% of GEODATA-positive pixels
carry below half occupancy of CMSA and CM-SSM respectively, while W10
block-mean cover correlations remain 0.73--0.87. Two bookkeeping gaps
are explicit rather than patched: 125.2\,km$^2$ of GEODATA-positive
pixels inherit no province label and are reported only in national
tallies, and 19--39\,km$^2$ of mapped extent per product lies beyond the
10\,km corridor and therefore outside domain-cell statistics. The audit
authorises no SILVER admission, no product ranking, and no label merge;
a multi-source 2020 training label would need an explicit conflict
rule, and the ten provisional cells remain outside any eligibility
decision. Full tables, the hypothesis-evidence JSON, and the candidate
figure ranking are versioned in
\texttt{2020\_label\_scale\_audit\_v1} and the companion analysis note.
