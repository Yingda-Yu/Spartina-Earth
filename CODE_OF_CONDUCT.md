# Code of Conduct

Spartina Earth is an open, long-term research project. We expect everyone
who contributes — in issues, pull requests, discussions, or elsewhere — to
keep the environment respectful and constructive.

## Standards

- Be welcoming and inclusive across backgrounds, institutions, and levels
  of experience.
- Respect differing viewpoints; debate ideas and evidence, not people.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus on what is best for the research and for scientific integrity.
- Do not harass, insult, or personally attack others.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
doxxing, and disruptive trolling.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that
violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict access to
project spaces. Reports can be sent privately to the Spartina Technology
maintainers via the repository owner's GitHub contact.
