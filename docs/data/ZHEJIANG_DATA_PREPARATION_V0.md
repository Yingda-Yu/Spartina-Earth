## 3. Season policy (v0, literature-grounded, locally NOT yet validated)

Comparing cross-year change requires the same phenological window. The
window below is the v0 *target*; it is a hypothesis for Zhejiang until
validated against local field/UAV evidence.

Evidence reviewed (2026-09-30):

- Zhou et al. 2024, *Remote Sensing* 16(8):1377, Yangtze River Delta
  1990–2022: *S. alterniflora* greens up later (lower NDVI Apr–May) and
  stays green into November while other vegetation wilts.
  doi:10.3390/rs16081377
- Zhang X. et al. 2026, 《湿地科学》 24(1):1–10, Xiangshan Gang, Zhejiang:
  combines the Jun–Jul vigorous window with the Dec–Jan senescence
  window to suppress reed/tidal-creek confusion.
  doi:10.13248/j.cnki.wetlandsci.20240083
- Ouyang et al. 2013, *PLoS ONE* 8(6):e67315: germination/early growth
  and flowering are the most separable stages in the Yangtze estuary.
  doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0067315
- Cao et al. 2026, *Int. J. Digital Earth* 19:1, Hangzhou Bay salt
  marshes: DOY 160–360 is the transferable window over 1986–2023.
  doi:10.1080/17538947.2026.2677965

Policy:

| Parameter | v0 value |
|---|---|
| Target window (primary) | **DOY 260–305** (~17 Sep – 1 Nov), late-greenness contrast |
| Backup window | **DOY 152–212** (~1 Jun – 31 Jul), vigorous canopy/flowering |
| Maximum within-year target drift | ±20 days before falling back to backup window |
| Cross-year rule | same window family every year; mixing autumn vs summer across years is forbidden and flagged |
| Minimum clear ROI coverage for scene selection | see `SingleScenePolicy` (0.99 footprint / 0.95 valid / 0.30 cloud) |

The autumn window is chosen first because the senescence contrast
against *Phragmites* is repeatedly reported; the Jun–Jul window remains
the fallback where cloud cover destroys autumn coverage. Every chosen
scene records its exact acquisition date so window compliance is
auditable. Nothing here is presented as a validated optimum for the
three bays.

## 4. Tide / inundation policy

Tidal state is a first-class confounder on mudflats. Policy:

1. Per scene, always record acquisition UTC and the v1
   `tide/inundation` fields from `src/spartina/data/gee/tide.py`.
2. Until a real gauge feed is connected, every value carries
   `method: "PROXY"`, `gauge_observed: false`, and a `proxy_basis`
   string. A proxy field must never be renamed to "observed tide".
3. Candidate real-gauge sources (all **TODO_VERIFY**, no data pulled):
   the national/ Zhejiang marine observation network stations relevant
   to these bays (e.g. Ganpu/Zhenhai for Hangzhou Bay; Kanmen for the
   central coast; Wenzhou for the south). Station identifiers, datum
   (85 national elevation / theoretical depth datum), and data license
   must be recorded before ingestion.
4. Selection must not mix acquisitions with obviously different
   inundation states without recording the mix; inundation stratification
   (exposed vs submerged mudflat) is an analysis variable, not a reason
   to silently drop scenes.

## 5. Dual-resolution science streams

| Stream | Era | Pixel | Backbone | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stream L** | 1984–present | 30 m native | Landsat 5/7/8/9 C2 L2 | Cross-decade long-term products |
| **Stream H** | 2015–present | 10 m native | S2 SR Harmonized (+ S1 at native spacing) | High-resolution era products |

- Overlap years (2015+) keep **both** streams; no forced unification.
- Landsat 30 m is never resampled to a 10 m grid and labelled 10 m
  (hard guard: `assert_no_forced_upsampling`).
- S1 GRD stays native until an experiment specifies a target GridSpec;
  any resampling is recorded in processing provenance (method, CRS,
  resampler).
- Cross-sensor alignment uses shared lattice origins from
  `covering_grid`, documented transform, and per-product grid hashes.
